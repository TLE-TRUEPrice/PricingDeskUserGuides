PricingDesk User Guide

Welcome to the TRUEPrice™ Pricing Desk User Guide!

This document describes how to interact with Pricing Desk to receive actionable pricing.

Steps to Price:

  • Log onto TRUEPrice
  • Select Pricing Desk from the Navigation Menu
  • Use the Account Information Tool below to fill out and export the Price Request Form
